How to Order

Ordering at Campus Bites takes four simple steps. No app and no account needed: just pick, pay, wait for your token and collect.

Order in 4 Steps

  1. 1

    Choose Your Items

    Look through the Menu and Deals pages, or the board above the counter. Decide what you want before you reach the front so the queue keeps moving.

    • Pick single items, or save money with a combo deal.
    • Tell the cashier about any allergies or special requests (for example, no onions or less spicy).
    • Ordering for friends? Give the whole order at once.
  2. 2

    Pay at the Counter

    Tell the cashier your order and pay right there. Prices are in PKR and include all taxes.

    • Cash is accepted.
    • Please keep small change ready where possible.
    • Check your receipt before you step away from the counter.
  3. 3

    Receive Your Token

    After payment you get a numbered token. This is your proof of order, so do not lose it.

    • Your token number is called out when your order is ready.
    • Find a seat or wait nearby while the kitchen prepares your food.
    • Usual wait is 5 to 10 minutes, longer at peak hours.
  4. 4

    Collect Your Order

    When you hear or see your number, go to the pick-up window and hand over your token. Check that everything is correct before you leave.

    • Collect from the pick-up window, not the payment counter.
    • Report any missing or wrong item immediately.

Peak-Hour Advice

The cafeteria is busiest when classes break. Plan around these times for a shorter wait.

Typical crowd levels through the day
Time Crowd Expected Wait
Opening to 9:30 AM Quiet 2 to 5 minutes
10:30 AM to 11:30 AM Moderate 5 to 10 minutes
12:30 PM to 2:00 PM Peak 15 to 20 minutes
3:00 PM to Closing Quiet 2 to 5 minutes

Tips to Beat the Rush

  • Come a little before or after the 12:30 to 2:00 PM lunch rush.
  • Know your order before joining the queue.
  • Go in a group and let one person order for everyone.
  • Short on time? Choose items that are quick to prepare, such as beverages, snacks and combos.
  • Keep exact change handy to speed up payment.

Pick-up Guidelines

  • Keep your token safe. Orders are handed over only against the matching token.
  • Collect promptly. Hot food is best eaten fresh. Uncollected orders are held for 15 minutes, after which they may be discarded without refund.
  • Check before you leave. Any issue with your order must be reported at the window right away.
  • Lost token? Speak to the cafeteria staff with your receipt. We will do our best to help.
  • Queue fairly. Please do not skip the line or collect on behalf of others without their token.
  • Keep it clean. Return trays and dispose of waste in the bins provided.
